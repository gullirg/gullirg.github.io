I am a research assistant in Prof. Neil Burgess's [Space & Memory Group](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/icn/research/research-groups/space-memory) at UCL.

My academic path has been deliberately interdisciplinary: I hold a BA in Philosophy from Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele in Milan, a BSc in Theoretical Physics from King's College London, and an MSc in Machine Learning from UCL. What ties it all together is a deep fascination with understanding how human thought and consciousness can arise from the brain's matter.

## Research & Experience

During my time at KCL I was an Undergraduate Research Fellow — awarded the **Research Excellence Award** — investigating robustness of Turing patterns in reaction-diffusion systems on complex networks. I also worked as a Material Analyst Technician at **Fermilab**, studying surface roughness in nitrogen-doped SRF cavities. At the **Hebrew University of Jerusalem** I interned with the Burak Lab, developing a ring attractor network model of place cells.

## Entrepreneurship

While studying Physics I co-founded **Mappin Technologies**, a startup building indoor navigation, asset tracking, and contagion monitoring tools. We were part of the **Kings20** and **Geovation** accelerator cohorts.

## Interests

At the intersection of neuroscience, physics, and machine learning, I am particularly interested in computational models of spatial cognition — how grid cells, place cells, and related circuits give rise to cognitive maps and memory.
